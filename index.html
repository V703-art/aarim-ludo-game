How to Play

  • 🎲 Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your base.
  • Click a highlighted token to move it.
  • Tokens travel around the 52-square outer track.
  • Each colour has its own 5-square home lane.
  • You must roll the exact number required to reach HOME.
  • ⭐ Coloured star spaces are safe spaces.
  • Tokens on safe spaces cannot be captured.
  • Landing on an opponent outside a safe space sends them back to BASE.
  • Rolling a 6 gives you another turn.
  • Capturing an opponent gives you another turn.
  • Reaching HOME gives you another turn.
  • Get all 4 tokens HOME to win.

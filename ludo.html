How to Play

  • 🎲 Roll a 6 to bring a token out of BASE.
  • Click a highlighted token to move it.
  • Tokens travel around the 52-square outer track.
  • Each colour has its own 5-square home lane.
  • You need the exact number to reach HOME.
  • ⭐ Coloured stars are safe spaces.
  • Tokens on safe spaces cannot be captured.
  • Landing on an opponent sends them back to BASE.
  • Rolling a 6 gives another turn.
  • Capturing gives another turn.
  • Reaching HOME gives another turn.
  • Get all 4 tokens HOME to win.
